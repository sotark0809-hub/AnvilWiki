**Last Updated: October 30, 2026 (Enshrouded 1.0, Hotfix 1.0.0.3)**

Vorgoth is the final boss of Enshrouded's main story — the encounter that concludes Embervale's tale and unlocks Flame Level 11, the highest Flame tier in the game. The fight waits at The Ancient Citadel, a fortress perched on the floating islands at the top of Cinder's Cradle, behind one of the longest quest chains in the campaign.

This guide covers how to unlock the fight, what is confirmed about its two forms, how to prepare, every known drop, and what changes after the credits roll — plus the full eight-boss chain that gates the game's Flame upgrades.

## Where is Vorgoth, and how do you unlock the fight?

Vorgoth waits at The Ancient Citadel, on the uppermost floating island of Cinder's Cradle — the endgame biome introduced in 1.0, which stacks a molten wasteland floor beneath a ring of ancient sky islands linked by Shroud vines. Community guides place the region in reportedly level 40+ territory, so this is the campaign's final stretch.

The fight itself is gated behind the "Cinders Of The End" quest chain, handed out by Loraine Crim, the Ancient Researcher — one of the new survivor NPCs introduced in 1.0. Its requirements are steep:

- Complete the "Land Of The Sky Islands" quest line.
- Clear all six Beacon Dungeons.

Community guides peg the full chain at roughly an hour of gameplay for a level-43 character (reportedly), so plan it as a session of its own rather than a quick detour.

## What are Vorgoth's two forms?

According to post-1.0 community guides, Vorgoth fights in two named forms: first as "Vorgoth, Champion of the Shroud", and then as "Vorgoth, the Wanderer's Vessel".

That is the shape of what is confirmed so far. Exact HP and phase health thresholds are still [TBC]; no reliable source has documented those numbers yet. The per-form attack lists, however, have now been described by both the enshrouded.tools database and community guides — see the table below. Treat the fight as a prolonged, multi-form finale rather than a burst check, and expect a long encounter at the end of the hour-long quest chain that precedes it.

| Form | Attacks community sources describe | How players answer it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form 1 — Champion of the Shroud (human-sized) | Ice greatsword swings, lunges and jumps, sweeping frost shockwaves across the floor, summoned Shroud wraiths and tentacle adds | Dodge the shockwave lines, clear the adds before refocusing the boss, punish the recovery after lunges |
| Form 2 — The Wanderer's Vessel (huge tentacled Shroud creature) | Frost eruptions across the arena, sweeping energy beams, ground shockwaves, tentacle strikes, projectiles, constant minion spawns | Keep moving, fight from range — reference footage used wand projectiles — and bring Fire damage |

*Sources (⚠️ community/database figures, not official): allthings.how's Vorgoth walkthrough (October 19, 2026) and bisecthosting.com's boss guide; the enshrouded.tools database adds that both forms take 120% damage from Fire and none from Shroud, with boss HP rising +70% per extra player in co-op.*

## How should you prepare for the Vorgoth fight?

Preparation is mostly about arriving at the top of your power curve, because this is the campaign's final check. The basics:

- **Level and gear.** The character level cap is 50 and gear goes to level 55 in 1.0. The quest chain's reference run sits at level 43, so the low-to-mid 40s with endgame gear is the realistic entry point.
- **Flame level 10.** Vorgoth gates the Flame 10 → 11 upgrade — the final step of the Flame chain — so you should already be at Flame 10 when you arrive.
- **A tuned build.** You can respec freely at a Flame Altar with Runes. Community 1.0 tier lists reportedly favor Archer/Ranger and Battlemage at the top, but Update 8 made every archetype viable: heavy attacks deal double damage and build stun, and armor mitigates up to 80% of incoming damage since the cap was raised from 60%.
- **Supplies for a long session.** The unlock chain alone reportedly takes about an hour, and the finale follows it — stock bandages, food, and ammunition accordingly.

## What does Vorgoth drop?

According to post-1.0 community guides, Vorgoth drops a signature legendary weapon and the two materials that gate the game's final Flame upgrade:

| Drop | Type | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shroud Vessel's Greatsword | Legendary two-handed sword | Reportedly a level 50/55 legendary greatsword — the fight's signature weapon |
| Vorgoth's Bound Essence | Boss material | One of the two Vorgoth materials required for Flame Level 11; also used to craft the Soulbound armor set |
| Vorgoth's Eye | Boss material | The second Vorgoth material required for Flame Level 11 |
| Legendary Runes | Currency | High-tier currency for endgame gear upgrades |

All of the above comes from community documentation rather than official patch notes, so treat quantities and drop behavior as reportedly accurate.

## What are Vorgoth's drops used for?

The short answer: the final Flame upgrade and the game's top-tier armor set — and they compete for the same materials.

### The Flame Level 11 upgrade

Flame Level 11 is the final Flame tier, unlocked by defeating Vorgoth. Reportedly, the full upgrade requirements at the Flame Altar are:

- 18 survivors housed with Sheltered Beds
- 20 Spark
- 10 Celestium Bars
- 15 Moonstone
- 20 Diamond Dust
- 15 Ember Carapace
- 1× Vorgoth's Bound Essence
- 1× Vorgoth's Eye

Flame 11 officially unlocks Shroud Removal and raises the Flame Altar cap from 15 to 16.

### Vorgoth's Soulbound armor set

Reportedly, the five-piece "Vorgoth's Soulbound" legendary armor set is crafted at Loraine Crim, with each piece costing one Vorgoth's Bound Essence — and the Crown piece additionally requiring 8 Magma Gel, 6 Moonstone, 1 Celestium Bars, and 1 Bound Essence. Note the conflict: because every piece consumes a Bound Essence and the Flame 11 upgrade needs one too, you cannot max the armor set and upgrade your Flame from a single kill. Bosses in the game respawn, so a second kill is the straightforward way to fund another piece.

## What happens after you beat Vorgoth?

Beating Vorgoth officially unlocks Flame Level 11, which enables Shroud Removal and raises your Flame Altar cap to 16. According to community write-ups of the ending, the finale plays out as a final conversation with the Flame, an illustrated narration outro, and a "QUEST COMPLETED! Cinders Of The End" banner — reportedly, as Keen Games has not documented the scene in detail.

One boundary reportedly stays in place: even after Shroud Removal, the red Deadly Shroud remains inaccessible. And in the studio's own words, with 1.0.0.0 "the story of Embervale is concluded - for now" — leaving the door open for whatever comes next.

## What is the full main-story boss order in Enshrouded?

Eight main bosses gate the Flame upgrades from level 2 to level 11, with one exception: the Flame 9 → 10 step requires a relic instead of a boss head. Here is the full chain:

| # | Boss | Location | Flame upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scavenger Matron* | Springlands, Hill of Scavengers | 2 → 3 |
| 2 | Fell Wispwyvern | Revelwood, Pikemead's Reach | 3 → 4 |
| 3 | Fell Monstrosity* | Nomad Highlands, Cradle of Dusk | 4 → 5 |
| 4 | Fell Sicklescythe* | Kindlewastes, Haunted Sun Temple | 5 → 6 |
| 5 | Fell Cyclops ("Evil Eye")* | Albaneve Summits | 6 → 7 |
| 6 | Fell Dragon Youngling | Albaneve Summits, Howling Peak | 7 → 8 |
| 7 | Hydrak'Dal* | Veilwater Basin | 8 → 9 |
| — | No boss — requires the Broken Guard Of The North Crown relic* | — | 9 → 10 |
| 8 | Vorgoth (final boss) | Cinder's Cradle, The Ancient Citadel | 10 → 11 |

Rows marked with an asterisk come from community boss lists published after 1.0 and should be read as reportedly accurate; the unmarked rows — Fell Wispwyvern, Fell Dragon Youngling, and Vorgoth — are the firmly confirmed anchors of the chain. Spare boss heads reportedly double as golden base trophies that grant +4 comfort.

## Vorgoth FAQ

### Is Vorgoth the final boss of Enshrouded?

Yes. Defeating Vorgoth concludes the main story added in 1.0 — officially "for now" — and unlocks Flame Level 11.

### How do you unlock the Vorgoth fight?

Take the "Cinders Of The End" quest chain from Loraine Crim in Cinder's Cradle, which requires completing "Land Of The Sky Islands" and clearing all six Beacon Dungeons.

### What level should you be for Vorgoth?

The quest chain's reference run sits at level 43, and the game's caps are level 50 with gear at 55 — so the low-to-mid 40s with endgame equipment is the realistic window.

### What are Vorgoth's drops used for?

Vorgoth's Bound Essence and Vorgoth's Eye are required for the Flame Level 11 upgrade, and the Bound Essence also crafts the five-piece Vorgoth's Soulbound legendary armor set at Loraine Crim.

### Can you enter the Deadly Shroud after Shroud Removal?

Reportedly no — community guides say the red Deadly Shroud stays inaccessible even with Shroud Removal unlocked.
