## Which build is strongest in Enshrouded 1.0?

The Ranger and the Battlemage share the top spot: expertgamereviews' 1.0 ranking puts the Ranger first, while Hack the Minotaur crowns the Battlemage—so this list ranks them as joint S tier rather than declaring a single winner. Barbarian, Wizard, and the new Summoner follow in A tier, with Warrior, Tank, and Healer rounding out B tier.

## How this tier list was made

This ranking aggregates 1.0-era community build guides and tier lists, weighing four factors: damage efficiency, survivability, ease of use, and value in solo versus co-op play. Two sources matter most: expertgamereviews' nine-build ranking (October 21, 2026) and Hack the Minotaur's tier content, and they disagree on the #1 spot. Where sources diverge, we say so instead of inventing a tiebreaker. Balance may shift with future patches, and since you can respec at Flame Altars with Runes, switching builds is always an option.

## What changed the meta in Update 8 and 1.0?

The 1.0 rankings look different from late-Early-Access lists for three reasons: every skill tree was reset, Update 8 rebuilt combat fundamentals, and 1.0 introduced an entirely new playstyle.

First, the reset. The official 1.0 changelog confirms all skill trees were wiped at launch—"hopefully for the last time"—so every build was re-planned from zero, and early 1.0 guides reflect those fresh routes rather than accumulated tweaks.

Second, Update 8 ("Forging the Path") changed what wins fights. Focus now fuels a Special Ability for every weapon type, heavy attacks deal double damage and build the enemy stun bar, and the armor damage-reduction cap jumped from 60% to 80%. Community guides report that the higher cap brought heavy armor back into the meta, which props up the Barbarian in A tier and keeps the Tank relevant in group content.

Third, weapon economics shifted. Wands reportedly top the damage-efficiency charts because they regenerate mana passively—the core reason the Battlemage sits in S tier. The bow had been weakened by its earlier skill tree rework, but guides report it recovered in 1.0 after Eternal arrows switched to mana costs, keeping the Ranger on top. Meanwhile the staff still lacks a dedicated Special Ability, which reportedly caps the Wizard below S. And the new Summoner kit—Advanced Necromancy plus Totem Mastery—earned its A-tier slot as the launch's headline new playstyle.

## Why each build earned its rank

- **Ranger (S)** — expertgamereviews' #1 for 1.0. A bow-first kit with daggers as the sidearm gives safe ranged damage plus mobility, and community guides report the bow recovered in 1.0 after Eternal arrows switched to mana costs.
- **Battlemage (S)** — Hack the Minotaur's #1. Wands reportedly top the damage-efficiency charts thanks to passive mana regen, the shield covers mistakes, and 1.0's new Blast and Cast combo adds burst.
- **Barbarian (A)** — A direct Update 8 winner: double-damage heavy attacks fill the stun bar, and the 80% armor mitigation cap makes heavy armor like the Sunpiercer set viable.
- **Wizard (A)** — Top-end burst, but reportedly the squishiest build in the game, and the staff's missing dedicated Special Ability remains a known weakness.
- **Summoner (A)** — The new archetype of the 1.0 launch. Advanced Necromancy raises Hollow Skeletons while Totem Mastery powers Fire, Ice, and Shock totems; community guides report it as a strong new playstyle.
- **Assassin (A)** — Daggers plus bow can rival anything, but per community consensus it leans hard on positioning and timing, which prices out many players.
- **Warrior (B)** — Middle-of-the-pack raw performance, yet reportedly the most beginner-friendly build in the game: one-handed weapon, shield, and Battle Heal sustain.
- **Tank / Guardian (B)** — The Golden Bulwark frontline shines in co-op, but solo its damage output trails the higher tiers.
- **Healer (B)** — The Waveborne Ritualist support kit is valuable in full groups and slow everywhere else.

## Will this tier list change after future patches?

Almost certainly. The current consensus dates to the 1.0 launch window and Hotfix 1.0.0.3; any balance patch that touches weapon damage, Focus abilities, or the armor cap can reorder the middle of the list. The saving grace is the respec system: a Flame Altar visit with Runes converts yesterday's meta pick into tomorrow's without penalty.

## FAQ

### What is the best class in Enshrouded?

There is no official ranking, and community sources disagree: expertgamereviews ranks the Ranger first in 1.0, while Hack the Minotaur puts the Battlemage at #1. We list both in S tier. Enshrouded also has no locked classes, so "best class" really means "best build."

### What is the best build for beginners?

The Warrior is reportedly the most beginner-friendly pick thanks to shield survivability and Battle Heal. The Ranger is the usual alternative for players who prefer fighting at range.

### Is the Summoner worth trying in 1.0?

Yes. It is the new playstyle introduced by the 1.0 launch—Advanced Necromancy plus Totem Mastery—and community guides report it as a strong addition rather than a gimmick, which is why it lands in A tier.

### Can I switch builds later?

Yes. Respec at any Flame Altar by spending Runes, then re-allocate points across all 12 skill trees. That makes tier-list churn cheap to follow.

### Is this tier list official?

No. It is a community-driven ranking based on public 1.0-era guides, current as of Hotfix 1.0.0.3 (October 21, 2026). Keen Games has not published official build tier data.
